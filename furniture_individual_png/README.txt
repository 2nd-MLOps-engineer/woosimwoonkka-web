Furniture PNG assets

48 level assets: 6 categories x 8 levels.
6 category icons are included in category_icons/.
All PNGs have transparent backgrounds and 8 pixels of outer padding.

Background extraction used the built-in image editing tool.
Prompt: Preserve the furniture, remove the paper, grid, labels, floors and external shadows, retain opaque object interiors, and output actual transparency.
Each level was then sliced to its own PNG. The Lv.1 rug uses the prior original-image cutout to retain its lettering.
